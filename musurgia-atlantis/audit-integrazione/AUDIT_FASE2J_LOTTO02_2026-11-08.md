## Conflitti e omonimie da preservare
1. **Jacopo Corsi**: Use DBI 1602-12-29; 1931 encyclopedia's ca.1604 is superseded. Fonti: https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/iacopo-corsi_res-5512502d-87eb-11dc-8e9d-0016357eee51_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/ ; https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/iacopo-corsi_%28Enciclopedia-Italiana%29/.
2. **Franz von Suppé**: 1819, not self-reported 1820; catalog spelling Suppè, variant Suppé. Fonti: https://musiklexikon.ac.at/0xc1aa500d_0x0001e405.
3. **Scott Joplin**: Birth between 1867 summer and January 1868 per Library of Congress, despite catalog 1868 label. Fonti: https://www.loc.gov/item/n50039880/scott-joplin-1868-1917/.
4. **John Williams**: Film composer John Towner Williams (1932) distinct from classical guitarist John Christopher Williams (1941). Fonti: https://www.bso.org/works/a-toast ; https://www.allmusic.com/artist/john-williams-mn0000815705.
5. **Sibelius**: IRCAM narrative has apparent internal slip 'wedding in 1882' after events in 1892; do not import unsupported statement. Fonti: https://medias.ircam.fr/en/composer/jean-sibelius/biography.
6. **Rudolf Friml**: Rose-Marie 1924 music by Friml AND Herbert Stothart; text Harbach and Hammerstein II. Fonti: https://natlib.govt.nz/records/38049112.
7. **Vecchi and Banchieri**: Polyphonic madrigal comedy not first opera or automatic precursor relation to monody; distinguish Banchieri baptismal Tommaso. Fonti: https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/orazio-tiberio-vecchi_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/ ; https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/adriano-banchieri_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/.
